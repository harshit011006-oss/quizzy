ROMANTIC QUIZ

Files:
- index.html
- style.css
- script.js

Run:
1. Put all 3 files in the same folder.
2. Open index.html directly, or serve the folder from your Express app.
3. The quiz has 6 questions and gives a score out of 6.
4. Score 5 or 6 => "You can claim one gift"
5. Score 0-4 => "Behh wait till 12 now 😭"

The question options are randomized each time, but the correct answers remain fixed.
Progress does not need a backend.
